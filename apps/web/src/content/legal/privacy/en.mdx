## 2. Personal data collected

- Profile picture (optional)

### 2.2 Contact data

- Email address
- Phone number (with country code)

### 2.3 Connection and browsing data

- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Pages visited and visit duration

### 2.4 Land project data

- Project type (acquisition, verification, training)

- Geographic area of interest in Cameroon

For Users accessing the KAMBRIQ LANDS Catalogue or entering into a Land Support Mandate, additional data is collected as part of anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing efforts:

- Copy of identity document (passport or ID card)
- Proof of address less than 3 months old
- Sworn declaration regarding the lawful origin of funds
- Additional supporting documents (bank statements, employment contracts, notarised deeds) for transactions exceeding 10,000 euros (or FCFA equivalent)

### 2.6 Communication data

- Messages exchanged with KAMBRIQ via the Platform, by email or by instant messaging (WhatsApp)
- Recordings of video calls (only with prior explicit consent)
- Internal commercial notes taken by KAMNET Agents

### 5.1 Primary storage

Data is stored on AWS servers located in **Frankfurt, Germany** (European Union), which provides GDPR-compliant protection for data collected from Users residing in the EU.

### 5.2 Transfers to Cameroon

KAMBRIQ teams located in Cameroon (Dschang, Douala-Dibamba, Yaoundé) access data in connection with their duties. - **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC)** of the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914)
- Internal confidentiality undertakings signed by each team member with access to data
- Appropriate technical and organisational measures (encryption, access controls, logging)

### 5.3 Information request

You can request a copy of the applicable SCCs or further information on the safeguards in place by writing to **contact@kambriq.com**.

## 7. Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR and applicable laws, you have the following rights over your personal data:

### 7.1 Right of access (art. 15 GDPR)

You can obtain confirmation as to whether your data is (or is not) being processed by KAMBRIQ and, where applicable, request a copy of that data.

### 7.2 Right to rectification (art. 16 GDPR)

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

### 7.3 Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (art. 17 GDPR)

You can request the deletion of your data in the cases provided for by the GDPR (in particular when the data is no longer necessary for the purpose, or if you withdraw your consent). This right is not absolute and may be limited by legal obligations (in particular KYC, accounting).

### 7.4 Right to restriction of processing (art. 18 GDPR)

You can request the restriction of processing (temporary suspension) in certain cases (contestation of accuracy, unlawful processing, etc.).

### 7.5 Right to data portability (art. 20 GDPR)

You can receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and transmit it to another data controller.

### 7.6 Right to object (art. 21 GDPR)

You can object, at any time, to processing based on KAMBRIQ's legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation. You have an absolute and unconditional right to object to processing for direct marketing purposes.

### 7.7 Right to withdraw consent (art. 7.3 GDPR)

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 7.8 Right relating to automated decisions (art. 22 GDPR)

KAMBRIQ does not take automated decisions producing legal effects on you or significantly affecting you.

### 7.9 Right to set post-mortem directives

For Users residing in France, in accordance with article 85 of the amended French Data Protection Act, you can set directives concerning the retention, erasure and communication of your data after your death.

## 9. Data security

KAMBRIQ implements **appropriate technical and organisational measures** to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or accidental disclosure.

### 9.1 Technical measures

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.2 or higher)
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- Regular backups
- Access monitoring and logging
- Regular security updates

### 9.2 Organisational measures

- Restriction of access to authorised personnel
- Confidentiality undertakings signed by each team member
- Staff awareness and training on data protection
- Security incident management procedure

### 9.3 Breach notification

In the event of a data breach likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, KAMBRIQ undertakes to inform you as soon as possible and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, in accordance with the GDPR.
