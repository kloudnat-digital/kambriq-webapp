# GDPR & Data protection by country

KAMBRIQ operates in several countries and complies with local data protection regulations.

## France

- **Regulatory framework:** GDPR (EU 2016/679)
- **Supervisory authority:** CNIL
- **Resource:** www.cnil.fr

KAMBRIQ fully complies with personal data processing obligations in this country, including rights of access, rectification and deletion.

## Belgium

- **Regulatory framework:** GDPR (EU 2016/679)
- **Supervisory authority:** APD
- **Resource:** www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be

KAMBRIQ fully complies with personal data processing obligations in this country, including rights of access, rectification and deletion.

## Canada

- **Regulatory framework:** PIPEDA / Law 25 (Québec)
- **Supervisory authority:** OPC
- **Resource:** www.priv.gc.ca

KAMBRIQ fully complies with personal data processing obligations in this country, including rights of access, rectification and deletion.

## Cameroon

- **Regulatory framework:** Law No. 2010/012
- **Supervisory authority:** ANTIC
- **Resource:** www.antic.cm

KAMBRIQ fully complies with personal data processing obligations in this country, including rights of access, rectification and deletion.
