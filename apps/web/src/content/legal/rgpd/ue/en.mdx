# Data protection notice - European Union

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It sets out the provisions applicable to Users residing in the **European Union** (in particular France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Germany and the Netherlands), in accordance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679)** and the national transposition laws.

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), is the controller of personal data collected through the dev.kambriq.com platform.

## 2. Your GDPR rights

As a resident of the European Union, you have the following rights over your personal data:

### 2.1 Right of access (article 15 GDPR)

You may obtain confirmation as to whether your data is being processed by KAMBRIQ and, where applicable, request a copy of that data.

### 2.2 Right to rectification (article 16 GDPR)

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data concerning you.

### 2.3 Right to erasure (article 17 GDPR)

You may request deletion of your data in the cases provided for by the GDPR. This right is not absolute and may be limited by legal obligations (in particular KYC/AML, 10-year accounting retention, ongoing litigation).

### 2.4 Right to restriction of processing (article 18 GDPR)

You may request restriction of the processing of your data in certain cases (challenge to accuracy, unlawful processing, pending objection, etc.).

### 2.5 Right to data portability (article 20 GDPR)

You may receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and transmit it to another controller.

### 2.6 Right to object (article 21 GDPR)

You may object, at any time, to processing based on the legitimate interest of KAMBRIQ, on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You have an **absolute and unconditional right to object** to processing for direct marketing purposes.

### 2.7 Right to withdraw consent (article 7.3 GDPR)

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 2.8 Rights related to automated decision-making (article 22 GDPR)

KAMBRIQ does not engage in automated decision-making producing legal effects concerning you or significantly affecting you.

### 2.9 Right to define post-mortem directives

For Users residing in **France**, in accordance with article 85 of the amended Loi Informatique et Libertés, you may define directives concerning the retention, erasure and disclosure of your data after your death.

## 3. How to exercise your rights

### 3.1 Request to KAMBRIQ

To exercise your rights, you may send your request:

- By email: **contact@kambriq.com**
- By post: Fotomena, Dschang, West Region, Cameroon

### 3.2 Response time

KAMBRIQ undertakes to respond within **one month** of receipt of your request. This period may be extended by 2 additional months for complex or numerous requests, with prior notice.

### 3.3 Free of charge

Exercising your rights is free of charge. KAMBRIQ may, however, charge reasonable fees for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests, in particular due to their repetitive nature.

## 5. National specificities

### 5.1 France - Specificities

- **Cookies**: In accordance with CNIL recommendations, the consent banner (forthcoming) will ensure that refusal is as easy as acceptance.
- **Telephone marketing**: Pursuant to article L.223-2 of the French Consumer Code, you may register on the Bloctel telephone marketing opposition list (www.bloctel.gouv.fr).
- **Post-mortem directives**: You may define general directives with a trusted third party certified by the CNIL, or specific directives with KAMBRIQ.

### 5.2 Belgium - Specificities

- The Belgian APD has extensive powers, including investigation and sanction.
- Prior mediation procedure is available with the APD's First-Line Service.

### 5.3 Germany - Specificities

- **Cookies**: German legislation is particularly strict on cookies (BGH Cookie-II case law). Any cookie not strictly essential requires explicit prior consent.
- **TTDSG** (Telekommunikation-Telemedien-Datenschutz-Gesetz) of 1 December 2021: specifically governs cookies and electronic communications.
- **Class actions**: Group action capability via consumer associations.

### 5.4 Netherlands - Specificities

- The Dutch AP applies a strict sanctions policy (substantial fines).
- Online complaint procedure directly accessible.

## 7. International data transfers

### 7.1 Primary storage in the EU

Your data is stored on AWS servers located in **Frankfurt, Germany** (European Union), ensuring direct application of the GDPR.

### 7.2 Transfers to Cameroon

KAMBRIQ teams in Cameroon (Dschang, Douala-Dibamba, Yaoundé) access data as part of their missions.

As Cameroon is not recognised as a country offering an adequate level of protection by the European Commission within the meaning of article 45 GDPR, these transfers are governed by:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** - Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021
- Internal confidentiality undertakings signed by each team member with access to data
- Additional technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit and at rest, strictly limited access controls, logging, staff awareness)

### 7.3 Additional information

You may request a copy of the applicable SCCs or further information on the safeguards in place by writing to **contact@kambriq.com**.
