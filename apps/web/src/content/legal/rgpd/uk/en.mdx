# Data protection notice - United Kingdom

## Preamble

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It sets out the provisions applicable to Users residing in the **United Kingdom**, in accordance with the **UK GDPR** (UK General Data Protection Regulation) and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), is the controller of personal data collected through the dev.kambriq.com platform.

## 1. Applicable legal framework

### 1.1 Reference texts

- **UK GDPR** (UK General Data Protection Regulation) - the British version of the GDPR following Brexit
- **Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)** - UK implementing legislation
- **Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR)** - governs cookies and electronic communications
- **Data Protection and Digital Information Act 2024** (where applicable, subject to legislative developments)

### 1.2 Post-Brexit continuity

Following the United Kingdom's withdrawal from the European Union on 31 January 2020, the European GDPR was incorporated into UK law as the "UK GDPR". The principles and rights are **substantially identical** to the EU GDPR, with some procedural adaptations specific to the United Kingdom.

## 2. Your rights (UK GDPR)

As a resident of the United Kingdom, you have the following rights:

### 2.1 Right of access (article 15 UK GDPR)

You have the right to obtain confirmation that your data is being processed and a copy of the data.

### 2.2 Right to rectification (article 16 UK GDPR)

You have the right to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.

### 2.3 Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (article 17 UK GDPR)

You have the right to request deletion of your data in certain circumstances. This right is not absolute and may be limited by legal obligations (KYC/AML, accounting records, ongoing litigation).

### 2.4 Right to restriction of processing (article 18 UK GDPR)

You have the right to restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.

### 2.5 Right to data portability (article 20 UK GDPR)

You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

### 2.6 Right to object (article 21 UK GDPR)

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, for reasons relating to your particular situation.

You have an **absolute right to object** to processing for direct marketing purposes.

### 2.7 Rights related to automated decision-making (article 22 UK GDPR)

KAMBRIQ does not engage in automated decision-making producing legal effects or significantly affecting you.

### 2.8 Right to withdraw consent (article 7.3 UK GDPR)

When processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

## 5. UK-specific provisions

### 5.1 Article 14 ePrivacy / PECR

The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) governs:

- Marketing emails, SMS, and phone calls
- Cookies and similar technologies
- Telephone preferences

KAMBRIQ complies with PECR for all communications directed at UK residents:

- Marketing communications: explicit opt-in consent required
- Cookies: see Cookie Policy at `/legal/cookies`
- TPS (Telephone Preference Service) / CTPS: KAMBRIQ does not engage in telephone marketing

### 5.2 Adequacy decision EU to UK

The European Commission adopted an adequacy decision on 28 June 2021, recognising the UK as offering an adequate level of data protection. Data flows between the UK and the EU therefore continue without additional safeguards (this decision is reviewed every 4 years).

### 5.3 Data transfers UK to outside UK

Transfers of UK personal data to third countries (including Cameroon) are governed by:

- **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)** - approved by Parliament in March 2022
- **UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses**

KAMBRIQ uses the **IDTA** to govern transfers from the UK to Cameroon.

## 7. International data transfers

### 7.1 Primary storage in EU

Your data is stored on AWS servers in **Frankfurt, Germany** (European Union). The EU benefits from a UK adequacy decision, allowing free data flow.

### 7.2 Transfers to Cameroon

KAMBRIQ teams located in Cameroon access your data as part of their missions. Cameroon is not recognised as a country offering an adequate level of protection by the UK government. These transfers are governed by:

- **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)**
- Internal confidentiality undertakings signed by each team member with access to data
- Technical and organisational safeguards (encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, logging, staff training)

### 7.3 Additional information

You may request a copy of the IDTA or further information by writing to **contact@kambriq.com**.

## 8. Brexit transition - alignment with EU GDPR

KAMBRIQ applies the **highest standard** between UK GDPR and EU GDPR for all UK Users. In practice:

- All EU GDPR rights are recognised for UK Users
- UK-specific provisions (PECR, IDTA, ICO authority) are added
- KAMBRIQ monitors the evolution of UK data protection law (especially any divergence from EU GDPR)
