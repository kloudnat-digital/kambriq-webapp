# Privacy Notice - California (USA)

## Preamble

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It sets out the provisions applicable to residents of the **State of California**, in accordance with the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** as amended by the **California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)** which became fully effective on January 1, 2023.

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**" or "**we**"), is the controller of the personal information collected through the platform dev.kambriq.com.

## 1. Legal framework

### 1.1 Reference texts

- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** of 2018, effective January 1, 2020
- **California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)** of 2020, fully effective January 1, 2023
- **California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)** for online privacy disclosures
- **Shine the Light Law** (California Civil Code §1798.83) for direct marketing disclosures

### 1.2 Applicability to KAMBRIQ

The CCPA/CPRA applies to KAMBRIQ if:
- KAMBRIQ collects personal information from California residents, AND
- KAMBRIQ meets at least one of the following thresholds:
  - Annual gross revenues over $25 million
  - Buys, sells, or shares personal information of 100,000+ consumers/households
  - Derives 50%+ of annual revenues from selling/sharing personal information

Although KAMBRIQ may not currently meet these thresholds, **KAMBRIQ voluntarily applies CCPA/CPRA standards** for all California Users to ensure transparency and high data protection.

## 6. Your rights under CCPA/CPRA

As a California resident, you have the following rights:

### 6.1 Right to know (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.110, §1798.115)

You have the right to request:
- The categories of personal information collected
- The categories of sources from which information is collected
- The business purposes for collecting information
- The categories of third parties with whom information is shared
- The specific pieces of personal information collected about you

### 6.2 Right to delete (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.105)

You have the right to request deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to exceptions (legal obligations, contract performance, fraud prevention, etc.).

### 6.3 Right to correct (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.106) - CPRA

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.

### 6.4 Right to opt-out of sale or sharing (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.120)

You have the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. **KAMBRIQ does not sell or share personal information**, so this right is automatically respected.

### 6.5 Right to limit use of sensitive personal information (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.121) - CPRA

You have the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. KAMBRIQ does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

### 6.6 Right to non-discrimination (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.125)

KAMBRIQ may not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights (no service refusal, price increase, quality reduction).

### 6.7 Right to data portability (CPRA)

You have the right to receive your personal information in a portable, technically feasible, and readily usable format.

Exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights is free of charge for up to **2 requests per year**. Beyond that, reasonable fees may apply for excessive requests.

### 7.5 Authorized agent

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. The agent must provide written authorization and proof of identity.
