# Data protection notice - Other countries (Global)

## Preamble

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It applies to Users residing in **countries not covered by a specific notice**, in particular the United Arab Emirates, Japan, China, Cameroon, and any other country hosting a Cameroonian diaspora.

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), is the controller of personal data collected through the dev.kambriq.com platform.

## 1. KAMBRIQ commitment - GDPR standard by default

KAMBRIQ undertakes to apply the **General Data Protection Regulation standard (GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679)** to all its Users, regardless of their place of residence, **unless local law provides higher protection** (in which case local law prevails).

This universal commitment reflects KAMBRIQ's intent to:

- Guarantee consistent and high-level protection to all Users
- Facilitate the management of relationships with a worldwide diaspora
- Anticipate the evolution of national legal frameworks towards stricter standards

## 2. Countries specifically covered by this Notice

### 2.1 United Arab Emirates

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Main framework | Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 - Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) |
| Authority | UAE Data Office |
| Website | u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data |

KAMBRIQ applies the PDPL for Users residing in the UAE. Your rights include access, rectification, erasure, objection, and portability, similar to the European GDPR.

### 2.2 Japan

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Main framework | Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) |
| Authority | Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC) - 個人情報保護委員会 |
| Website | www.ppc.go.jp |
| Specificities | EU-Japan adequacy decision in force since 2019 |

Japan benefits from a European Commission adequacy decision, facilitating data transfers. KAMBRIQ complies with the APPI for Users residing in Japan, including:

- The opt-in principle for the processing of sensitive data
- Rights of access, rectification, suspension of use
- Notification obligations in case of serious breaches

### 2.3 China

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Main framework | Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) of 2021 |
| Authority | Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) |
| Website | www.cac.gov.cn |
| Specificities | Strict restrictions on international transfers |

The Chinese PIPL imposes strict obligations regarding international transfers. KAMBRIQ informs its Users residing in China that:

- Processing of their data involves transfers to the European Union (Frankfurt) and Cameroon
- These transfers are governed in accordance with PIPL requirements (specific consent, contractual mechanisms)
- Your rights include access, rectification, copy, withdrawal of consent, and restriction

### 2.4 Cameroon

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Main framework | Law No. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybersecurity and cybercrime |
| Authority | Agence Nationale des Technologies de l'Information et de la Communication (ANTIC) |
| Website | www.antic.cm |
| Specificities | KAMBRIQ has its registered office in Cameroon; ANTIC declaration to be made after creation of the SARL |

For Users residing in Cameroon, processing is subject to Cameroonian law. The ANTIC declaration will be made upon official registration of the SARL.

### 2.5 Other EU countries not specifically covered

The other European Union countries (Italy, Spain, Portugal, Austria, Poland, Ireland, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Czech Republic, Hungary, etc.) are subject to the same GDPR framework as the countries explicitly covered by the `/legal/rgpd/ue` Notice. Local authorities are the national data protection authorities of each country (Garante in Italy, AEPD in Spain, CNPD in Portugal, etc.).

### 2.6 West Africa and other African countries

Several African countries host a Cameroonian diaspora (Ivory Coast, Senegal, Benin, Togo, etc.). These countries generally have national data protection laws aligned with international standards:

- **Ivory Coast**: Law 2013-450 of 19 June 2013, ARTCI authority
- **Senegal**: Law 2008-12 of 25 January 2008, CDP authority
- **Benin**: Law 2017-20 of 20 April 2018, APDP authority
- **Togo**: Law 2019-014 of 29 October 2019, IPDCP authority

KAMBRIQ applies the GDPR standard for these Users, supplemented by local requirements where they are more protective.

### 2.7 Other countries - Generic clause

For any other country not specifically listed, KAMBRIQ applies the **GDPR standard by default**, unless local law is more protective (in which case local law prevails).

If you reside in a country with specific data protection legislation and wish to benefit from it, contact **contact@kambriq.com**. KAMBRIQ will examine your request and apply the more protective provisions applicable to your situation.

## 3. Your universal rights

Whatever your country of residence, KAMBRIQ guarantees you at minimum the following rights (GDPR standard):

### 3.1 Right of access

You may obtain confirmation as to whether your data is being processed by KAMBRIQ and request a copy of the data.

### 3.2 Right to rectification

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data concerning you.

### 3.3 Right to erasure

You may request deletion of your data in the cases provided for by applicable law, subject to legal exceptions (KYC/AML, accounting retention, litigation).

### 3.4 Right to restriction of processing

You may request the temporary restriction of the processing of your data.

### 3.5 Right to data portability

You may receive your data in a structured format and transmit it to another controller.

### 3.6 Right to object

You may object to the processing of your data on legitimate grounds, and unconditionally for direct marketing purposes.

### 3.7 Right to withdraw consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

## 4. How to exercise your rights

## 7. International data transfers

### 7.1 Primary storage in the EU

Your data is stored on AWS servers located in **Frankfurt, Germany**, ensuring protection aligned with the GDPR.

### 7.2 Transfers to Cameroon

KAMBRIQ teams in Cameroon access your data as part of their missions. KAMBRIQ governs these transfers through:

- Internal confidentiality undertakings signed by each team member

You may request further information on the safeguards in place by writing to **contact@kambriq.com**.
