# Data protection notice - Switzerland

## Preamble

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It sets out the provisions applicable to Users residing in **Switzerland**, in accordance with the **revised Federal Act on Data Protection (nLPD/FADP)** which entered into force on **1 September 2023**, and its implementing ordinance (OPDo).

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), is the controller of personal data collected through the dev.kambriq.com platform.

## 1. Applicable legal framework

### 1.1 Reference texts

- **Federal Act on Data Protection of 25 September 2020 (nLPD/FADP)**, in force since 1 September 2023
- **Data Protection Ordinance (OPDo)** of 31 August 2022
- **Telecommunications Act (LTC)** for electronic communications

### 1.2 EU-Switzerland adequacy decision

The European Commission adopted an adequacy decision recognising Switzerland as offering a level of data protection equivalent to that of the EU (decision of 26 July 2000, with an update forthcoming following the nLPD). Data flows between Switzerland and the EU therefore benefit from a simplified framework.

## 2. nLPD fundamental principles

The nLPD is based on the following principles:

- **Lawfulness**: data is processed lawfully
- **Good faith and proportionality**: processing is proportionate to the purpose pursued
- **Purpose limitation**: data is processed only for the purpose indicated at collection
- **Accuracy**: data must be accurate and up to date
- **Security**: adequate protection against unauthorised processing
- **Transparency**: clear information to data subjects
- **Privacy by design / by default**: protection built in from the design stage

## 3. Your rights (nLPD)

As a resident of Switzerland, you have the following rights:

### 3.1 Right of access (article 25 nLPD)

You may obtain confirmation as to whether your data is being processed by KAMBRIQ and request a copy of the data concerning you.

### 3.2 Right to rectification (article 32 nLPD)

You may request the correction of inaccurate data concerning you.

### 3.3 Right to erasure and destruction (article 32 nLPD)

You may request the erasure or destruction of your data in the cases provided for by the nLPD. This right is not absolute and may be limited by legal obligations.

### 3.4 Right to data portability (article 28 nLPD)

You may receive your data free of charge in a common electronic format or request its transmission to another controller.

### 3.5 Right to object

You may object to the processing of your data on legitimate grounds relating to your particular situation.

You have an absolute right to object to processing for direct marketing purposes.

### 3.6 Rights related to automated decision-making (article 21 nLPD)

KAMBRIQ does not engage in automated individual decision-making producing legal effects concerning you or significantly affecting you.

### 3.7 Right to withdraw consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 3.8 High-risk profiling (Swiss specificity)

The nLPD introduces the specific concept of **"high-risk profiling"**, which refers to automated processing intended to evaluate certain personal aspects of an individual and entailing a high risk to that person's personality or fundamental rights.

KAMBRIQ does **not** engage in high-risk profiling.

## 6. Swiss specificities

### 6.1 Criminal sanctions (nLPD novelty)

<Callout type="warning">
Unlike many regimes (GDPR, UK GDPR), the nLPD provides for **criminal sanctions** in case of serious violations (up to CHF 250,000 in fines). KAMBRIQ pays particular attention to full compliance with the nLPD.
</Callout>

### 6.2 Notification of security breaches

In accordance with article 24 nLPD, KAMBRIQ notifies the FDPIC of any data security breach entailing a high risk to the personality or fundamental rights of data subjects, as soon as possible. Data subjects are also informed if necessary.

### 6.3 Cross-border data communication (articles 16-18 nLPD)

The nLPD strictly regulates cross-border data communications. KAMBRIQ governs transfers to Cameroon through:

- Standard contractual clauses recognised by the FDPIC
- Equivalent internal safeguards (undertakings, training)
- Transparent information to data subjects

### 6.4 Data Protection Officer (DPO)

The nLPD permits (but does not currently require KAMBRIQ to make) the appointment of a Data Protection Officer. KAMBRIQ is considering this appointment as part of the official registration of the SARL.

The competent Swiss courts are, in principle, those of the claimant's domicile or those of the controller's registered office.
