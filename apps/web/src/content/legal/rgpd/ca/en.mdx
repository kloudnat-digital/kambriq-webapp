# Data protection notice - Canada / Quebec

## Preamble

This Notice supplements the **Privacy Policy** of KAMBRIQ available at `/legal/privacy`. It sets out the provisions applicable to Users residing in **Canada** (federal framework) and more specifically in **Quebec** (enhanced provincial framework).

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with registered office in Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), is the controller of personal data collected through the dev.kambriq.com platform.

## 1. Applicable legal framework

### 1.1 Canadian federal framework

- **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA / LPRPDE)** of 2000
- **Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL / LCAP)** of 2014, governing commercial electronic communications
- Bill C-27 introducing the **Consumer Privacy Protection Act (CPPA / LPVPC)** - currently before Parliament

### 1.2 Quebec provincial framework

- **Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector**, amended by **Quebec Law 25** (An Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information), brought into force progressively from 2022 to 2024

Quebec Law 25 is considered **substantially equivalent to the European GDPR**, and even stricter on certain aspects.

### 1.3 EU-Canada adequacy (PIPEDA)

In 2001 the European Commission adopted an adequacy decision recognising that PIPEDA offers an adequate level of protection for data transfers from the EU to Canadian private-sector organisations subject to PIPEDA.

## 2. Your rights

As a resident of Canada (and particularly Quebec), you have the following rights:

### 2.1 Right of access

You may obtain confirmation as to whether your data is being processed by KAMBRIQ and request a copy of the data concerning you.

### 2.2 Right to rectification

You may request correction of inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous data concerning you.

### 2.3 Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Quebec Law 25)

For residents of **Quebec**, Law 25 introduces a right to de-indexing and a right to cessation of dissemination. You may request:

- Erasure of your data in the cases provided for by law
- Cessation of the dissemination of your information
- De-indexing of a hyperlink where it causes serious harm and the dissemination contravenes the law

### 2.4 Right to data portability (Quebec Law 25, since 22 September 2024)

For residents of **Quebec**, you may receive your personal information in a structured and commonly used technological format.

### 2.5 Right to object

You may object to the processing of your information for direct marketing purposes or on grounds relating to your particular situation.

### 2.6 Rights related to automated decision-making (Quebec Law 25)

If a decision is made concerning you exclusively on the basis of automated processing, you have the right:

- To be informed of it
- To obtain the information used to make the decision
- To submit observations to a staff member who may review the decision

KAMBRIQ does not currently make automated decisions producing significant effects on Users.

### 2.7 Right to withdraw consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

## 5. Canadian specificities

### 5.1 Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL)

CASL strictly governs commercial electronic communications (emails, SMS) in Canada:

- **Explicit prior consent** required for prospecting communications
- Clear identification of the sender
- Effective unsubscribe mechanism in every message

KAMBRIQ complies with CASL for all communications directed at Canadian residents.

### 5.2 Quebec Law 25: enhanced obligations

Quebec Law 25 imposes obligations more demanding than federal PIPEDA:

- Mandatory designation of a **Person in Charge of the Protection of Personal Information** (currently fulfilled by the Publication Director, Mr Visquis MIAFFOSSA)
- Privacy impact assessments (PIAs)
- Mandatory notification of confidentiality incidents to the CAI Commissioner and to data subjects
- Dissuasive financial penalties (up to CAD 25 million or 4% of worldwide turnover)

### 5.3 Notification of confidentiality incidents

KAMBRIQ undertakes to notify confidentiality incidents presenting a serious risk of harm:

- To the federal OPC and/or the CAI as applicable
- To affected data subjects
- As soon as possible

## 7. International data transfers

### 7.2 Transfers to Cameroon

KAMBRIQ teams in Cameroon access data as part of their missions. In accordance with Quebec Law 25 (article 17), KAMBRIQ ensures that information benefits from **equivalent protection** to that provided by Quebec law when communicated outside Quebec, through:

- Prior privacy impact assessment (PIA)

- Technical and organisational measures (encryption, access controls, logging)
