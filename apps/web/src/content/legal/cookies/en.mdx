## 1. What is a cookie?

A **cookie** is a small text file placed on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. It allows the site to recognise you, remember some of your preferences, or collect information about your browsing.

**Similar technologies** include invisible pixels, web beacons, fingerprinting, and any other device that allows information to be stored on or read from your device.

### 1.1 Types of cookies by duration

- **Session cookies**: erased when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies**: kept until their expiry date or manual deletion

### 1.2 Types of cookies by origin

- **Internal (first-party) cookies**: placed directly by dev.kambriq.com
- **Third-party cookies**: placed by third-party services integrated into the Platform

## 2. Categories of cookies

KAMBRIQ classifies cookies into the following categories, in line with the recommendations of the CNIL and the European Data Protection Board (EDPB):

### 2.1 Strictly necessary cookies (essential)

These cookies are **indispensable to the operation of the Platform**. They cannot be disabled and do not require your prior consent, in accordance with article 82 of the amended French Data Protection Act and the ePrivacy Directive.

In particular, they:

- Maintain your logged-in session
- Ensure connection security (CSRF tokens)
- Manage the cart or in-progress form
- Balance load between servers
- Remember your cookie preferences (recorded consent)

### 2.2 Functional cookies

These cookies improve your user experience by remembering certain preferences:

- Display language
- Selected region or country
- Display preferences (light / dark mode, font size)
- Pre-filling of non-sensitive forms

These cookies are placed with your consent.

### 2.3 Audience measurement cookies

These cookies allow KAMBRIQ to understand how visitors use the Platform, for anonymised statistical analysis and service improvement.

**At this time, no audience measurement cookie is deployed on the Platform.**

KAMBRIQ is considering the future deployment of an audience measurement tool (in particular Google Analytics or an equivalent tool such as Matomo or Plausible). Upon deployment, this Policy will be updated and a granular consent banner will be put in place to allow you to accept or refuse these cookies on an informed basis.

### 2.4 Advertising and marketing cookies

These cookies would allow advertising or marketing content to be personalised.

**KAMBRIQ does not deploy advertising or marketing cookies on the Platform.** The KAMBRIQ business model relies on personalised support via a dedicated commercial representative, not on behavioural advertising.

### 2.5 Social network cookies

These cookies are placed by social sharing buttons or social modules embedded in some sites.

**At this time, no active social sharing module or button is integrated into the Platform.** Links to KAMBRIQ accounts on social networks (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp) are simple external links that do not place cookies until you click on them.

## 4. Your choices regarding cookies

### 4.1 Consent banner (upcoming)

Upon future deployment of audience measurement tools or other non-essential cookies, KAMBRIQ will implement a **granular consent banner** allowing you to:

- Accept all cookies
- Refuse all non-essential cookies
- Customise your choices by category

The banner will comply with CNIL and EDPB recommendations:

- Refusal will be as easy as acceptance
- No non-essential cookie will be placed before your explicit consent
- Your choices will be stored for a maximum of 6 months
- You may change your choices at any time

## 5. Cookies and personal data

### 5.1 Relationship with the Privacy Policy

The processing of personal data potentially collected via cookies is governed by the **Privacy Policy** accessible at page `/legal/privacy`.

KAMBRIQ undertakes to:

- Place non-essential cookies only with your consent
- Respect your rights over your data (access, rectification, erasure, objection)
- Limit the retention period of data collected via cookies

### 5.2 International transfers

When cookies place data on servers located outside the European Union (for example, Google Analytics whose servers are in the United States), KAMBRIQ ensures that appropriate safeguards are in place:

- European Commission adequacy decisions where applicable
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) of the European Commission
- Additional measures (encryption, anonymisation) where necessary

At this time, no cookie transfers outside the EU take place (essential cookies are placed and read locally by the AWS Frankfurt servers located in the EU).
