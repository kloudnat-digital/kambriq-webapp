# KYC and Anti-Money Laundering Charter - KAMBRIQ

## Preamble

KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), supports its clients (hereinafter the "**Clients**") in the framework of land transactions in Cameroon. Given the nature and amounts usually involved in these transactions, KAMBRIQ undertakes to apply the most demanding standards in matters of customer knowledge (Know Your Customer, hereinafter "**KYC**") and the fight against money laundering and terrorist financing (Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing, hereinafter "**AML/CFT**").

This Charter sets out the KYC/AML principles, obligations and procedures applicable to any contractual relationship between KAMBRIQ and a Client, in accordance with the international standards of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF), the directives of the Central African Economic and Monetary Community (CEMAC), and the applicable legislation (Cameroonian, European, international).

## 1. Legal framework and applicable standards

### 1.1 International standards

KAMBRIQ aligns with the following standards:

- **FATF Recommendations** (Financial Action Task Force), 40 updated recommendations
- **Mérida Convention** (United Nations Convention against Corruption)
- **Vienna Convention** (United Nations Convention against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs)
- **International Conventions against the Financing of Terrorism**

### 1.2 CEMAC regional framework

- **Regulation No. 02/22/CEMAC/UMAC/CM** on combating money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation
- Directives of the Bank of Central African States (BEAC) on international transfers

### 1.3 Cameroonian national framework

- **Law No. 2010/021** on combating money laundering and terrorist financing
- **Law No. 2010/012** on cybersecurity and cybercrime
- Implementing texts of the **National Financial Investigation Agency (ANIF)**

### 1.4 European Union framework (EU resident Clients)

- **Directive (EU) 2015/849** (4th AML directive), modified by the 5th directive 2018/843 and the 6th directive 2018/1673
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1624** (AML package 2024 - applicable progressively)
- National transposition legislation (in particular, in France, the Monetary and Financial Code and anti-money laundering through TRACFIN)

### 1.5 OHADA framework

- **OHADA Uniform Act on the organisation of general commercial law**
- OHADA provisions relating to anti-money laundering in commercial transactions

## 2. Guiding principles

### 2.1 Principle of systematic enhanced due diligence

Given the nature of the land transactions supported by KAMBRIQ and the amounts usually involved (plots of several tens to several hundreds of millions of FCFA), KAMBRIQ applies **enhanced due diligence to all of its Clients**, regardless of any financial threshold.

This systematic approach is justified by:

- The strategic nature of land assets (high unit value)
- The international origin of funds (global diaspora)
- Exposure to risks identified by the FATF in the real estate sector
- KAMBRIQ's commitment to transparency and integrity

### 2.2 Principle of cooperation

KAMBRIQ fully cooperates with the competent authorities (ANIF, ANTIC, authorities of the Client's country of residence, international authorities) in the event of a legitimate request in the framework of an investigation into money laundering or terrorist financing.

### 2.3 Principle of proportionality

KYC/AML measures applied are proportionate to the risk identified and the nature of the service. Vigilance may be reinforced in the event of:

- Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)
- Countries or jurisdictions identified as high-risk by the FATF
- Complex or unusual financial schemes
- Doubts about the lawful origin of funds

### 2.4 Principle of confidentiality

KAMBRIQ processes the KYC information collected with the strictest confidentiality, in accordance with the Privacy Policy accessible at `/legal/privacy`.

## 3. Mandatory KYC procedure

### 3.1 Client identification

Before any contractual relationship (signing of a Verification Mandate, an Advisory Mandate, or access to the KAMBRIQ LANDS Catalogue), KAMBRIQ proceeds with Client identification by collecting the following documents:

#### 3.1.1 Natural persons

- Copy of a valid official ID document (passport or national identity card with legible photograph)
- Proof of address less than 3 months old (electricity, gas, water or telephone bill, or bank attestation)
- Proof of professional activity (where applicable)
- Valid email address and phone number

#### 3.1.2 Legal entities

- Extract from the trade register (Kbis or equivalent) less than 3 months old
- Up-to-date articles of association
- Identification of beneficial owners (any natural person directly or indirectly holding more than 25% of capital, or exercising equivalent control)
- ID documents of directors and beneficial owners (per section 3.1.1)
- Power of attorney of the signing representative

### 3.2 Verification of the origin of funds

For any contractual relationship involving financial transactions (in particular within the framework of a Land Advisory Mandate), the Client provides:

- A **sworn declaration** of lawful origin of the funds engaged
- **Supporting evidence of the origin of funds**, for example:
  - Bank statements over 3 to 6 months
  - Pay slips or employer attestation
  - Asset sale contracts (real estate, movable property, shares)
  - Notarial deed of donation or inheritance
  - Proof of company sale
  - Any other document demonstrating the lawful traceability of funds

### 3.3 Risk profile verification

KAMBRIQ assesses the Client's risk profile taking into account:

- The Client's geographical situation and that of the transaction
- The Client's professional activity
- The amount and nature of the contemplated operation
- Any qualification as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or family member / close associate of a PEP
- Any presence on international sanctions lists

## 4. Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)

### 4.1 Definition

The following are considered Politically Exposed Persons (PEP):

- Heads of State, government, ministers, deputy ministers
- National parliamentarians
- Members of supreme and constitutional courts
- Members of courts of auditors
- Ambassadors and chargés d'affaires
- General officers of the armed forces
- Members of governing bodies of public companies
- Senior officials of international organisations

Their close family members (spouse, children, parents, parents-in-law) and persons known to be closely associated with them are also considered as PEP.

### 4.2 Enhanced PEP due diligence

For Clients identified as PEP or assimilated persons, KAMBRIQ implements the following measures:

- Prior approval of the Publication Director before the start of the contractual relationship
- Enhanced vigilance on the origin of funds (detailed and cross-checked supporting evidence)
- Continuous monitoring of the relationship and transactions
- Periodic re-examination of the file (every 6 months)

## 5. International sanctions

### 5.1 Systematic verification

KAMBRIQ systematically verifies, before any contractual relationship, that the Client does not appear on the following sanctions lists:

- United Nations lists (Sanctions Council)
- European Union lists (targeted sanctions)
- US OFAC lists (Specially Designated Nationals - SDN)
- United Kingdom consolidated list (HM Treasury)
- Relevant lists of other jurisdictions according to the context

### 5.2 Refusal of the relationship

Any person identified on an international sanctions list is denied access to KAMBRIQ services. KAMBRIQ informs the competent authorities in the event of an attempted contractual relationship with a sanctioned person.

## 6. Refusal, suspension and termination

### 6.1 Cases of initial refusal

KAMBRIQ may refuse to enter into a contractual relationship in the following cases:

- Refusal by the Client to provide the required KYC documents
- Documents provided that are incomplete, illegible, falsified or suspicious
- Origin of funds not demonstrated or suspicious
- Identification of the Client as a sanctioned person
- Serious doubt about the legitimate purpose of the operation
- Manifest inconsistencies in the Client's declarations

### 6.2 Cases of suspension during the relationship

KAMBRIQ may suspend the performance of a Mandate in the event of:

- Suspicious evolution of the Client's profile
- Appearance of new elements (international sanctions, ongoing investigation)
- Refusal by the Client to provide additional information requested
- Attempt by the Client to fragment or conceal operations

### 6.3 Cases of termination

KAMBRIQ may unilaterally terminate the contractual relationship, without compensation and without notice, in the event of:

- Confirmation of unlawful origin of funds
- Listing of the Client on an international sanctions list during the relationship
- Refusal by the Client to cooperate with the competent authorities
- Serious breach of KYC/AML obligations

In these cases, KAMBRIQ retains the fees received corresponding to the services actually performed and may be required to report the operation to the competent authorities.

## 7. Suspicious activity report

### 7.1 Principle

In accordance with legal anti-money laundering obligations, KAMBRIQ is required to report to the competent authorities (in particular to ANIF in Cameroon, TRACFIN in France, or any other competent financial intelligence unit) any suspicious operation within the meaning of applicable regulations.

### 7.2 Confidentiality of the report

In accordance with the law, the Client cannot be informed of any suspicious activity report concerning them, nor of the action taken by the authorities. This confidentiality obligation is binding on KAMBRIQ.

### 7.3 Legal protection

KAMBRIQ and its representatives cannot be civilly, criminally or disciplinarily prosecuted for suspicious activity reports made in good faith to the competent authorities.

## 8. Data retention

### 8.1 Retention period

KYC documents and documents relating to operations are retained for **5 years from the end of the contractual relationship** with the Client, in accordance with legal anti-money laundering obligations.

### 8.2 Retention modalities

Documents are retained in secure electronic format, on AWS Frankfurt (eu-central-1) servers used by KAMBRIQ. Access is strictly limited to authorised personnel.

### 8.3 Secure destruction

At the expiration of the retention period, documents are securely destroyed (definitive deletion, without possibility of recovery).

## 9. Training and internal organisation

### 9.1 Personnel training

KAMBRIQ personnel and KAMNET Agents are trained on the applicable KYC/AML obligations. Training is:

- Initial (upon onboarding or certification)
- Periodic (annual update at minimum)
- Adapted to regulatory developments

### 9.2 AML referent

The **Publication Director** (Visquis MIAFFOSSA) currently assumes the functions of AML referent for KAMBRIQ. Any alert or question relating to anti-money laundering is addressed to him.

A more formalised AML arrangement will be put in place as soon as the SARL is officially registered (designation of an AML correspondent formally declared to the authorities).

### 9.3 Internal procedures

KAMBRIQ formalises and keeps up-to-date internal procedures covering:

- Identification and assessment of AML risk by client and by operation
- Validation of contractual relationships with PEPs
- Suspicious activity report modalities
- Document retention
- Personnel training

## 11. Client information

### 11.1 Transparency commitment

KAMBRIQ informs its Clients of:

- The existence of this KYC/AML Charter
- The KYC documents that will be requested from them
- The purpose of the data collection
- The retention periods
- Their rights over their personal data (see Privacy Policy)

### 11.2 Information modalities

Information is delivered:

- On the Platform dev.kambriq.com (page `/legal/kyc-aml`)
- Before the signature of any Mandate (express presentation to the Client)
- At any time, upon request sent to contact@kambriq.com

## 12. Evolution of the Charter

KAMBRIQ reserves the right to modify this Charter at any time, in particular to:

- Reflect regulatory developments (CEMAC, OHADA, EU, FATF)
- Adapt the arrangement to new KAMBRIQ activities
- Strengthen the level of protection according to the risks identified

The applicable version is the one published on the Platform at the date of signature of the Mandate. Substantial modifications are notified to Clients in active contractual relationships.
