# Land Advisory Mandate - KAMBRIQ

## Preamble

This Land Advisory Mandate (hereinafter the "**Mandate**") governs the conditions under which KAMBRIQ SARL (in formation), a limited liability company under Cameroonian law with its registered office at Fotomena, Dschang (hereinafter "**KAMBRIQ**"), supports a client (hereinafter the "**Client**") in completing a land acquisition in Cameroon.

This Mandate complements the Terms of Use (Terms) accessible at `/legal/terms`. In the event of conflict, the provisions of this Mandate shall prevail over the Terms.

<Callout type="danger">
**IMPORTANT WARNING**: KAMBRIQ supports the Client within the framework of a **best-efforts obligation**. The timeframes for completing land transactions in Cameroon depend largely on the land authorities (MINDCAF, land registry) and cannot be guaranteed by KAMBRIQ. Signature of this Mandate constitutes the Client's understanding and acceptance of this reality.
</Callout>

## Article 1 - Subject of the Mandate

By this Mandate, the Client entrusts KAMBRIQ with an advisory mission within the framework of a land acquisition project concerning an identified plot (hereinafter the "**Plot**") classified under one of the three KAMBRIQ Labels:

- **KAMBRIQ TFL™** (Titled and Subdivided Land) - immediate ownership
- **KAMBRIQ VEFL™** (Sale Pending Subdivision) - ownership after subdivision
- **KAMBRIQ VEFIL™** (Sale Pending Title and Subdivision) - ownership after title registration then subdivision

KAMBRIQ accepts this mission and undertakes to deploy reasonable and professional means to support the Client in completing the acquisition.

## Article 2 - Scope of the support

KAMBRIQ's support comprises the following services:

### 2.1 Presentation and selection of the Plot

- Presentation of the KAMBRIQ LANDS Catalogue to the Client (under confidentiality obligation)
- Advice on selection of the Plot according to the Client's criteria (geographical area, budget, use, time horizon)
- Transparent information on the applicable Label (TFL™, VEFL™, VEFIL™) and its implications

### 2.2 Prior verification

- Performance of the KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ service on the selected Plot, free of charge within the framework of this Mandate
- Delivery of the verification Report to the Client (see Verification Mandate, page `/legal/mandat-verify`)

### 2.3 Reservation and negotiation

- Connection of the Client with the seller-owner
- Support during the negotiation phase (price, conditions, timeframes)
- Formalisation of the Plot reservation (deposit, exclusivity)

### 2.4 Administrative support

- Preparation of files to be submitted to the land authorities
- Coordination with accredited surveyors (where applicable)
- Follow-up of the administrative steps specific to the Plot's Label:
  - **TFL™**: direct transfer via notarial deed
  - **VEFL™**: follow-up of the post-acquisition subdivision process
  - **VEFIL™**: follow-up of the title registration process then subdivision

### 2.5 Notarial support

- Recommendation of a Cameroonian notary registered with the Bar
- Preparation and coordination of the file submitted to the notary
- Presence at key steps (signature of the authentic deed)

### 2.6 Post-acquisition follow-up

- Follow-up of land registry inscription procedures (updated Land Title)
- Information of the Client on administrative developments
- For VEFL™ and VEFIL™ Labels: follow-up until the definitive Land Title is obtained

## Article 3 - Indicative timeframes by Label

The timeframes indicated below are **indicative timeframes** based on KAMBRIQ's average experience. They depend largely on the Cameroonian land authorities and cannot be guaranteed.

| Label | Indicative completion timeframe | Main steps |
|-------|----------------------------------|----------------------|
| **TFL™** | 2 to 4 months | Notarial deed + registry entry |
| **VEFL™** | 6 to 18 months | Notarial deed + subdivision process by MINDCAF |
| **VEFIL™** | 12 to 36 months | Notarial deed + title registration + subdivision |

<Callout type="warning">
These timeframes may be extended by administrative contingencies, third-party challenges, regulatory changes or cases of force majeure. KAMBRIQ regularly informs the Client of the progress of the file.
</Callout>

## Article 4 - KAMBRIQ fees

### 4.1 Fee structure

KAMBRIQ fees for this Mandate are structured as follows, in line with the responsibilities and duration of support specific to each Label:

#### 4.1.1 For TFL™ plots (simple flat fee)

Flat fees set according to the value of the Plot and the complexity of the file, communicated to the Client prior to signing the Mandate. As the transaction is immediate, the KAMBRIQ mission focuses on presentation, verification, negotiation and signature support phases.

#### 4.1.2 For VEFL™ and VEFIL™ plots (flat fee + follow-up)

Two-component fees:

- **Initial flat fee**: covering the presentation, verification, reservation, negotiation and signature of the notarial deed phases
- **Follow-up fees**: percentage of the transaction amount, due upon clearing key administrative steps (subdivision, title registration, issuance of the definitive Land Title)

The follow-up percentage and the triggering conditions are specified in the fee schedule annexed to the signed Mandate.

### 4.2 Payment terms

The fees are payable according to the following schedule:

- Down payment upon signature of the Mandate
- Balance of the initial flat fee upon signature of the notarial deed
- Follow-up fees (where applicable) upon clearing each key step

No online payment is currently available. Payments are made by bank transfer or any other means accepted by KAMBRIQ.

### 4.3 Ancillary costs

KAMBRIQ fees do not include:

- Notary fees
- Transfer duties and land taxes
- Surveyor fees (if not included in the acquisition price)
- Specific administrative costs (stamps, publication, etc.)
- The Plot acquisition price

These costs are borne directly by the Client and are communicated as early as possible by KAMBRIQ.

## Article 5 - Mandatory KYC procedure

### 5.1 Systematic enhanced due diligence

Given the nature of the land transactions supported by KAMBRIQ and the amounts usually involved (plots from several tens to several hundreds of millions of FCFA), KAMBRIQ systematically applies **enhanced due diligence** to all support files, in accordance with international standards on anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (FATF, OHADA, CEMAC).

### 5.2 Mandatory documents

Before the start of the mission, the Client provides KAMBRIQ with:

- Copy of valid ID document (passport or national identity card)
- Proof of address less than 3 months old
- Sworn declaration of lawful origin of funds
- Supporting evidence of origin of funds (bank statements, sale contracts, donations, inheritances, etc.)
- For legal entities: Kbis or equivalent, articles, identification of beneficial owners

### 5.3 Possible refusal

KAMBRIQ reserves the right to refuse the start or continuation of the mission in the event of:

- Refusal by the Client to provide the required KYC documents
- Serious doubt about the lawful origin of funds
- Person identified as a politically exposed person (PEP) without an appropriate vigilance arrangement
- Listing of the Client on an international sanctions list

In these cases, KAMBRIQ may terminate the Mandate without compensation, in accordance with the provisions of the KYC/AML Charter.

## Article 6 - Client obligations

The Client undertakes to:

- Provide KAMBRIQ with all information and documents necessary for the performance of the Mandate, in an accurate, complete and up-to-date manner
- Cooperate in good faith with KAMBRIQ and its team of KAMNET Agents
- Respect agreed appointments and deadlines
- Pay KAMBRIQ fees and ancillary costs at the agreed deadlines
- Inform KAMBRIQ of any significant change in the project or personal situation
- Respect the confidentiality of the KAMBRIQ LANDS Catalogue and information communicated
- Not bypass KAMBRIQ by dealing directly with sellers or agents identified through the mission

<Callout type="warning">
**Anti-circumvention clause**: The Client undertakes, during the duration of the Mandate and for 24 months after its end, not to finalise a transaction directly with a seller identified by KAMBRIQ, without paying KAMBRIQ the fees provided for in the Mandate. Any breach of this clause exposes the Client to the payment of the entire fees due to KAMBRIQ.
</Callout>

## Article 7 - KAMBRIQ obligations

KAMBRIQ undertakes to:

- Support the Client with professionalism, transparency and good faith
- Carry out the prior verifications (KAMBRIQ VERIFY™) on the selected Plot
- Regularly inform the Client of the progress of the file (regular progress reports)
- Alert the Client without delay in the event of any difficulty or identified risk
- Respect the confidentiality of information communicated by the Client
- Make a certified KAMNET Agent available to the Client as a dedicated contact
- Retain all file documents for the legal duration (10 years after the end of the Mandate)

## Article 8 - Best-efforts obligation

### 8.1 Principle

KAMBRIQ commits within the framework of a **best-efforts obligation** and not an obligation of result. KAMBRIQ cannot guarantee:

- The completion of the transaction (in particular in the event of refusal by the seller, the notary, or the authorities)
- Completion timeframes, which depend on land and notarial authorities
- The absence of future disputes with third parties
- A favourable evolution of the value of the Plot
- Regulatory and tax developments in Cameroon

### 8.2 Acknowledged contingencies

The Client expressly acknowledges the following contingencies, specific to the Cameroonian context:

- Variable and unpredictable administrative timeframes
- Risk of customary challenges (families, communities)
- Legislative or regulatory changes in land matters
- Difficulties accessing certain areas (rainy seasons, infrastructure)
- Variability of the FCFA / Euro / foreign currency exchange rate

KAMBRIQ undertakes to inform the Client of any significant contingency and to propose appropriate management solutions.

## Article 9 - Liability

### 9.1 KAMBRIQ liability

KAMBRIQ's liability is engaged in the event of a characterised breach of its contractual obligations, within the limits set out in the Terms (article 11).

### 9.2 Liability cap

KAMBRIQ's overall liability towards the Client under this Mandate is capped at the **amount of fees received** during the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to liability, without prejudice to the mandatory provisions of applicable laws.

### 9.3 Exemptions

<Callout type="warning">
KAMBRIQ shall not be held liable for:

- Decisions made by the Client despite KAMBRIQ's warnings
- The behaviour of the seller, the notary, the surveyor or any third party
- Cameroonian administrative, judicial or legislative contingencies
- Cases of force majeure
- Inaccurate information communicated by the Client
</Callout>

## Article 10 - Duration and termination

### 10.1 Duration of the Mandate

The Mandate takes effect upon signature and ends:

- For **TFL™** plots: upon signature of the authentic notarial deed and registration in the land register
- For **VEFL™** plots: upon issuance of the Land Title in the Client's name after subdivision
- For **VEFIL™** plots: upon issuance of the Land Title in the Client's name after title registration and subdivision

### 10.2 Termination for fault

Each Party may terminate the Mandate in the event of a serious breach by the other Party of its obligations, after a formal notice that has remained without effect for 30 days.

### 10.3 Amicable termination

The Parties may terminate the Mandate by mutual agreement, at any time. In this case, KAMBRIQ retains the fees received corresponding to the services actually performed.

### 10.4 Termination by KAMBRIQ for KYC reasons

KAMBRIQ may unilaterally terminate the Mandate, without compensation, in the event of:

- Refusal by the Client to provide the required KYC documents
- Serious doubt about the lawful origin of funds
- Listing of the Client on an international sanctions list
- Action by the Client aimed at circumventing KAMBRIQ or concealing information

### 10.5 Effects of termination

In the event of termination, KAMBRIQ delivers the file documents to the Client and retains the fees corresponding to the services actually performed. The confidentiality obligations and the anti-circumvention clause (article 6) survive termination.

## Article 11 - Dispute recourse

### 11.1 Internal claim

In the event of a dispute, the Client sends a reasoned claim to **contact@kambriq.com**. KAMBRIQ examines the claim and responds within 15 working days.

### 11.2 Prior mediation

Before any legal action, the Parties undertake to seek an amicable solution through mediation, in accordance with article 14.2 of the Terms.

### 11.3 Competent courts

Failing amicable resolution, any dispute shall be submitted to the competent courts of Yaoundé, Cameroon, without prejudice to the mandatory provisions applicable to consumers residing in the European Union.

For international disputes, the Parties may resort to arbitration before the Common Court of Justice and Arbitration of OHADA (CCJA).

## Article 12 - Personal data

The processing of the Client's personal data within the framework of this Mandate is governed by the Privacy Policy accessible at `/legal/privacy`. The Client acknowledges having read the said Policy.

KYC data collected in the framework of the mission is retained for 5 years from the end of the contractual relationship, in accordance with legal anti-money laundering obligations.

## Article 13 - Confidentiality

### 13.1 Mutual commitment

The Parties undertake to treat as strictly confidential all information exchanged within the framework of the Mandate.

### 13.2 Specific Client commitment

The Client undertakes in particular to:

- Not reproduce, distribute or publish the KAMBRIQ LANDS Catalogue or any extract
- Not disclose to third parties information about identified sellers and owners
- Not contact the KAMNET network directly without KAMBRIQ's intermediation

### 13.3 Duration

The confidentiality obligation subsists throughout the duration of the Mandate and for a period of 5 years after its end.

## Article 14 - Final provisions

### 14.1 Modification of the Mandate

No modification shall be valid unless formalised by a written amendment signed by the Parties.

### 14.2 Assignment

The Mandate may not be assigned by the Client without the prior written consent of KAMBRIQ. KAMBRIQ may assign the Mandate to any company of its group or to any purchaser of its business.

### 14.3 Governing law

The Mandate is governed by Cameroonian law and the applicable OHADA Uniform Acts.

### 14.4 Entire agreement

The Mandate, supplemented by the Terms, the Privacy Policy, the KYC/AML Charter, the Verification Mandate (where applicable) and the annexed fee schedule, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

## Article 15 - Acceptance

The signature of this Mandate (handwritten or electronic) by the Client constitutes full and complete acceptance of all its provisions, as well as of the Terms, the Privacy Policy and the KYC/AML Charter of KAMBRIQ.
