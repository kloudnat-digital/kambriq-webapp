## Article 7 - KAMBRIQ TFL™, VEFL™ and VEFIL™ Labels

### 7.1 Definition of Labels

KAMBRIQ classifies the land plots presented under three proprietary Labels:

- **KAMBRIQ TFL™ (Titled and Subdivided Land)**: plot whose registration has been completed (Land Title issued) AND whose subdivision is complete. Ownership transfers immediately upon signature of the notarised deed.
- **KAMBRIQ VEFL™ (Sale Pending Subdivision)**: plot whose registration has been completed (Land Title issued) BUT whose subdivision is in progress. Ownership is deferred until subdivision is completed.
- **KAMBRIQ VEFIL™ (Sale Pending Title and Subdivision)**: plot whose registration is in progress and whose subdivision will follow. Ownership is deferred until registration and then subdivision are completed. This is the Label with the longest path to ownership.

### 7.2 Notice

<Callout type="warning">
The KAMBRIQ TFL™, VEFL™ and VEFIL™ Labels are **internal classifications proprietary to KAMBRIQ** intended to provide transparent communication on the legal and documentary status of each plot. They do not replace the official legal qualifications established by Cameroonian land authorities (MINDCAF, land registry). The User acknowledges having understood the meaning of each Label and the associated risks, in particular for the VEFL™ and VEFIL™ Labels, which involve a deferred ownership timeline and a degree of administrative uncertainty.
</Callout>

### 7.3 Notarial recommendation

For any land acquisition in Cameroon, KAMBRIQ recommends that the User engage the services of a **Cameroonian notary registered with the Order**. Only the notary is competent to authenticate land transaction deeds and ensure the legal security of the operation.
