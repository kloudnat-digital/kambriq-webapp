## Article 1 - Subject of the Mandate

By this Mandate, the Client entrusts KAMBRIQ with a land verification mission concerning a plot identified by the Client (hereinafter the "**Plot**"), under the conditions set out in the following articles.

KAMBRIQ accepts this mission and undertakes to deploy reasonable and professional means to produce a verification report (hereinafter the "**Report**") that complies with the methodological standards of KAMBRIQ VERIFY™.

The Mandate does **not** constitute:

- A guarantee of the absolute authenticity of the land documents presented by the seller
- A guarantee against hidden defects or undetectable fraud
- A purchase or financing commitment by KAMBRIQ
- An authentic deed or notarial mission

## Article 2 - Scope of the 7 verifications

KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ comprises the following seven (7) verification points, performed by KAMBRIQ teams and certified KAMNET Agents:

### 2.1 Documentary verification of the Land Title

KAMBRIQ verifies with the competent land registry (MINDCAF):

- The authenticity of the Land Title presented
- The identity of the registered owner
- The possible existence of registered charges, mortgages or easements
- The conformity of the Land Title entries with the official registers

### 2.2 Cadastral verification

KAMBRIQ verifies:

- The consistency of the declared surface area with the cadastral documents
- The geographic coordinates of the Plot
- The site plan and its registration with the cadastre
- The agreement of boundaries between the documents and the official situation

### 2.3 Verification of the seller-owner

KAMBRIQ verifies:

- The seller's identity (ID document, supporting evidence)
- The seller's legal capacity to dispose of the property
- The seller's effective ownership right (consistency with the Land Title)
- The absence of non-consenting co-heirs or co-owners
- The possible existence of a mandate or power of attorney

### 2.4 Physical site visit

A certified KAMNET Agent travels to the site to:

- Verify the correspondence between the physical Plot and the land documents
- Observe the general state of the Plot (occupation, existing buildings, visible anomalies)
- Photograph the Plot and its boundaries
- Identify physical boundary markers where applicable

### 2.5 Neighbourhood verification

KAMBRIQ enquires with the neighbourhood and local authorities to identify:

- Any ongoing land disputes affecting the Plot or neighbouring parcels
- Apparent easements (passages, accesses, drainage)
- Known challenges to ownership
- The history of occupation and any customary conflicts

### 2.6 Urban planning verification

KAMBRIQ verifies with the urban planning services and the competent town hall:

- The zoning applicable to the Plot (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural use)
- The buildability (prior authorisations, permitted ratios)
- The subdivision plan validated by MINDCAF (where applicable)
- Public development projects likely to affect the Plot (roads, infrastructure)

### 2.7 Verification of the chain of ownership

KAMBRIQ verifies the history of transfers of the Plot:

- Successive transfers recorded
- The existence of an unbroken chain of ownership
- The absence of known historical disputes
- The conformity of transfers with OHADA transfer rules

## Article 3 - Methodology

### 3.1 Required physical visits

The KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ mission involves at minimum **two physical visits**:

- A visit to the **competent land registry** (MINDCAF) for documentary verifications
- A visit to the **Plot site** for the physical inspection and neighbourhood enquiry

These visits are essential and cannot be replaced by exclusively remote verification.

### 3.2 Administrative contingencies

The Client acknowledges that administrative verifications in Cameroon are subject to contingencies beyond KAMBRIQ's control:

- Availability of officials and registry consultation timeframes
- Sometimes delayed updating of official databases
- Variable processes for issuing administrative attestations

KAMBRIQ informs the Client of any significant delay in the progress of the mission.

### 3.3 Documents required from the Client

To start the mission, the Client must provide KAMBRIQ with:

- A copy of the Land Title (or registration receipt for VEFIL plots)
- The available site or subdivision plan
- The seller's contact details (identity, contact)
- The precise address of the Plot
- Any complementary document in possession (boundary survey report, certificate of ownership)

## Article 4 - Pricing and payment terms

### 4.1 Fee

The standard fee for the KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ service is **99 euros, all taxes included** (or equivalent in FCFA at the prevailing exchange rate), for a Plot located within Cameroonian territory.

### 4.2 Conditional waiver

The KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ service is offered **free of charge** to the Client when the Client subsequently signs, in continuity with the verification mission, a Land Advisory Mandate with KAMBRIQ for the acquisition of the verified Plot.

In that case, the 99-euro amount is credited against the Advisory fees.

### 4.3 Payment terms

Payment is made by bank transfer or any other means accepted by KAMBRIQ before the start of the mission. No online payment is currently available on the Platform.

### 4.4 Ancillary costs

The KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ fee covers ordinary administrative costs. Any ancillary costs (stamps, specific administrative duties, additional travel for very remote areas) are communicated to the Client before being incurred and invoiced separately.

## Article 5 - Delivery timeframe

### 5.1 Indicative timeframe

KAMBRIQ undertakes to deliver the Report to the Client within an indicative timeframe of **21 working days** from the complete receipt of the required documents and payment.

### 5.2 Causes of extension

The timeframe may be extended in the event of:

- Temporary unavailability of official registers or competent authorities
- Particular case complexity (complex chain of ownership, ongoing disputes)
- Difficulties accessing the Plot (remote areas, weather conditions)
- Need for additional investigations with neighbours or witnesses
- Force majeure

KAMBRIQ informs the Client of any significant delay and its cause.

### 5.3 Enforceability

The 21-working-day timeframe is an **indicative timeframe** and not a firm contractual deadline. Its overrun does not entitle the Client to compensation, except in the case of a characterised breach by KAMBRIQ.

## Article 6 - Content of the Report

### 6.1 Structure of the Report

The Report delivered by KAMBRIQ includes:

- A Plot identification sheet (cadastral references, coordinates, surface area)
- A summary of the 7 verifications performed
- For each verification: conclusion, observations where applicable, identified risk level
- A global synthesis note with final recommendation (favourable, favourable with reservations, unfavourable)
- The supporting evidence consulted

### 6.2 Risk scale

The Report classifies the Plot according to the internal KAMBRIQ risk scale:

- **Controlled**: no significant risk identified
- **Moderate**: identified risks but manageable with support
- **To monitor**: heightened vigilance required, points of attention listed
- **Not recommended**: significant legal or administrative risks

### 6.3 Confidentiality of the Report

The Report is strictly confidential and intended for the Client only. Its distribution to third parties is subject to the prior written authorisation of KAMBRIQ.

## Article 7 - Limits of the Mandate

### 7.1 Best-efforts obligation

KAMBRIQ commits within the framework of a **best-efforts obligation** and not an obligation of result. KAMBRIQ does not guarantee:

- The absolute absence of hidden defects on the Plot
- The absence of documentary fraud not detectable by standard verifications
- The lasting legal validity of the documents (in the event of subsequent judicial challenge)
- The absence of future disputes with third parties
- The completion of the land transaction (which depends on the Client, the seller, and the notary)

### 7.2 Excluded scope

The Mandate does not include:

- Authentication of notarial deeds (which falls to the notarial office)
- Drafting of legal acts (which falls to the lawyer or notary)
- Economic or commercial valuation of the Plot
- Technical expertise (geotechnical, environmental, archaeological)
- Post-verification follow-up (unless an Advisory Mandate is signed)

### 7.3 Notarial recommendation

KAMBRIQ systematically recommends that the Client engage the services of a **Cameroonian notary registered with the Bar** to authenticate the land transaction. Only the notary has competence to engage notarial liability for the formal regularity of the deed.
