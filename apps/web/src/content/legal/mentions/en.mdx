## 4. KAMBRIQ activity

KAMBRIQ is a **land strategy advisory firm** specialised in Cameroonian land matters. KAMBRIQ offers the following services:

- **KAMBRIQ LANDS**: presentation of verified land plots, support for land acquisition in Cameroon. Catalogue access is confidential and requires a prior conversation with a KAMBRIQ commercial representative.
- **KAMBRIQ VERIFY™**: verification service for land titles and third-party plots, based on 7 control points.
- **KAMBRIQ Business School (KBS)**: certifying training for land agents (KCA1, KCA2, KCA3 tracks).
- **KAMNET**: network of commercial agents certified by KAMBRIQ.

<Callout type="info">
KAMBRIQ is an **advisory firm**. KAMBRIQ is not a land seller, nor an exclusive agent of property owners. KAMBRIQ supports its clients in the selection, verification and securing of their land projects. Land transactions remain personal commitments between the buyer and the seller, formalised before a notary and in accordance with Cameroonian law.
</Callout>
