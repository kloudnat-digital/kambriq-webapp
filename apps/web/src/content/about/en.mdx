# About KAMBRIQ

## Our mission

KAMBRIQ supports the Cameroonian diaspora in acquiring land in Cameroon, transforming a historically opaque, risky, and stressful process into a transparent, methodical, and secure experience.

We believe that access to Cameroonian land should be as safe and accessible for the diaspora as it is for anyone, in any country. Our model rests on a simple promise: **every land we propose is verified, every label is documented, every risk is disclosed.**

---

## Our vision

### At 5 years

**Become THE transparency reference for Cameroonian land for the global diaspora.**

We want to make Cameroonian land an investment as safe and accessible as in any developed country. This requires uncompromising methodological rigor, full transparency on risks, and a strong operational presence on the ground in Cameroon.

### At 10 years

**Extend the KAMBRIQ model to land in other African countries.**

The African land market remains largely unstructured and massively exposes buyers (residents and diaspora alike) to avoidable risks. We aim to become the reference standard for land advisory services in sub-Saharan Africa, starting by extending our model to countries where the Cameroonian diaspora is already present: Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Benin, Togo, and beyond.

## Our five values

### 1. Transparency

We say everything, with no grey zones. Our labels **TFL™**, **VEFL™** and **VEFIL™** classify each land according to its actual legal status: immediate ownership, deferred until ongoing subdivision, or deferred until registration then subdivision. The risks associated with each label are stated explicitly, not buried in fine print.

### 2. Rigor

Our **KAMBRIQ VERIFY™** service rests on 7 systematic verification points: land title, cadastre, selling owner, physical visit, neighborhood, urban planning, chain of ownership. Every verification is documented. Every report delivered to the client is traceable. Our methods are public and auditable.

### 3. Proximity

We are physically present in Cameroon (head office in Dschang, operational representation in Douala-Dibamba, Yaoundé deployment in progress) and we maintain a human team attentive to the diaspora worldwide. No KAMBRIQ case is handled remotely only: our KAMNET Agents travel to every land, every land registry office, every town hall.

### 4. Integrity

We apply systematic enhanced KYC due diligence on every case. We refuse cases where the source of funds is not demonstrated, transactions involving sanctioned persons, or any circumvention of regulatory procedures. Our integrity is not negotiable, and that is precisely what protects our clients.

### 5. Excellence

Our **KAMNET** network is made up of agents certified by our in-house school **KAMBRIQ Business School (KBS)**, trained in KAMBRIQ methods and contractually committed to respecting our specifications. The quality of our cases, the rigor of our reports, and the precision of our advisory engagements are our signature.

## Our story

KAMBRIQ was born from a double observation: a human one and a market one.

### The human observation

Within the Cameroonian diaspora, land fraud is not an exception, it is an almost ordinary experience. Lands sold several times, fake land titles, fictitious subdivisions, sellers vanishing with the deposit: these stories circulate in every Cameroonian community in Paris, Brussels, Montreal, New York, London, Geneva, Dubai. They shatter life projects, divide families, and instill a lasting distrust of returning home.

We have seen too many relatives, acquaintances, and testimonies recount these stories. We understood that what was missing, between the diaspora buyer and Cameroonian land, was a structured, rigorous, transparent actor capable of doing the work no one does: verify, support, document, secure.

### The market observation

The Cameroonian land market represents considerable volume, but it remains structurally unprofessionalized: informal intermediation, absence of transparency standards, documentary opacity, extreme fragmentation of actors. The diaspora, on the other hand, has real investment capacity, but no reliable channel to engage.

KAMBRIQ fills this double gap: a structured access channel for the diaspora, and a professional standard for the market.

### Key milestones

- **2024**: Initial research, consultations with notaries, surveyors, land agents. Definition of the TFL™, VEFL™, VEFIL™ labels and the 7-verification methodology.
- **2025**: Model structuring, first lands in catalogue, first VERIFY™ pilot missions, formalization of KAMBRIQ Business School (KBS) and certification of the first KAMNET agents.
- **2026**: Production launch of the dev.kambriq.com platform, publication of the LANDS Catalogue (15+ verified lands), official incorporation of the Cameroonian SARL, deployment of multi-jurisdiction legal documentation (GDPR, national laws).

## Advisor is not seller

A distinction that grounds our model: **KAMBRIQ is a land strategy advisory firm**. We are neither sellers of lands nor exclusive agents of owners. We support our clients in selecting, verifying, and securing their land projects, but transactions remain personal contractual commitments between buyer and seller, formalized before a notary in accordance with Cameroonian law.

This distinction is essential for two reasons:

1. **Independence**: we are not commercially tied to any particular seller. Our interest is aligned with the client's, not the seller's.
2. **Methodological honesty**: a rigorous advisor may conclude that a land is not suitable, or that a case should be dropped. A seller cannot say that. An advisor must.
