## What we verify

Every land goes through 7 control points before entering our catalogue.

1. **Title deed authenticity** - Is the document genuine? Does it match official registries?
2. **Identity of the actual owner** - Is the seller the owner registered in the land registry?
3. **Known disputes** - Are there any litigation, objections, or competing claims on this land?
4. **Geographic accuracy** - Does the declared location match the cadastral plan and on-site boundary markers?
5. **Complete technical file** - Are the boundary survey, location plan, and cadastral plan compliant?
6. **Current legal status** - Is the land free of any mortgage, seizure, or easement?
7. **Transaction history** - Who sold what, when, and under what conditions?

## Our 3 security levels

Every verified land receives a KAMBRIQ label that clearly indicates its legal status.

### KAMBRIQ TFL™ - Titre Foncier Loti

The land has an individual title deed established. This is the highest security level. The transaction can proceed immediately.

### KAMBRIQ VEFL™ - Vente en État Futur de Lotissement

The title deed already exists - land registration is complete. Subdivision is in progress. You purchase with the certainty that an individual title deed will be issued once subdivision is complete. Ownership transfers once subdivision is complete. Timelines are shorter than VEFIL™.

### KAMBRIQ VEFIL™ - Vente en État Futur d'Immatriculation et de Lotissement

Land registration is in progress. Subdivision will follow registration. You are **NOT** the owner upon signing - you must wait for both land registration **AND** subdivision to complete. This is the longest of the three processes. KAMBRIQ accompanies you throughout with additional verification milestones.

> **KAMBRIQ VEFIL™ ≠ KAMBRIQ VEFL™.** On a VEFIL™ land, the buyer takes on higher risk. This is why KAMBRIQ requires additional verification milestones.

## What KAMBRIQ is NOT

KAMBRIQ is not a land seller.

KAMBRIQ is a **land strategy advisor**.

We do not own the lands we propose. Our role is to verify, secure, and guide your project - from your first contact to obtaining your title deed.
