# Our method - The 7 KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ checks

## Why 7 checks?

In Cameroon, secure land purchase does not rest on a simple reading of a land title. Sophisticated land fraud combines multiple angles: forged documents, unauthorized seller, poorly delimited land, customary disputes, unvalidated subdivision plan, broken chain of ownership.

To address this complexity, KAMBRIQ has structured its method around **7 systematic verification points**. Each point covers a distinct risk angle. No point substitutes for the others: security arises from their convergence.

---

## The 7 checks

### 1. Documentary verification of the Land Title

**Goal**: confirm the authenticity and relevance of the Land Title (TF) presented by the seller.

**What we verify**:

- The physical and formal authenticity of the TF (watermarks, signatures, registration references)
- The identity of the registered owner, as recorded at the land registry office
- Charges, mortgages, easements, and marginal entries on the TF
- The consistency between the TF entries and official registries (updates, transfers)
- The absence of seizure, opposition, or judicial decision restricting disposal of the property

**How**: physical visit to the competent land registry office (depending on the land's district), consultation of the registry, request for a recent mortgage status report.

**Risks covered**: forged TF, expired TF, TF encumbered with mortgages, TF under judicial dispute.

---

### 2. Cadastral verification

**Goal**: confirm that the physical land matches exactly the cadastral documents.

**What we verify**:

- Declared surface area vs. cadastral surface area
- Geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, UTM projection where applicable)
- The site plan and its registration with the cadastre
- The consistency of boundaries between documents and the official cadastral situation
- The absence of overlap with neighboring parcels

**How**: consultation of cadastral plans, comparison with topographic surveys (boundary survey, subdivision plan), on-site reconnaissance with a certified surveyor if necessary.

**Risks covered**: sale of a smaller land than declared, displaced boundary markers, "off-set" land (incorrect cadastral attachment), overlap with an already-titled parcel.

---

### 3. Verification of the selling owner

**Goal**: confirm that the seller is indeed the person authorized to convey the land.

**What we verify**:

- The identity of the seller (official ID, in current validity)
- Their legal capacity to dispose of the property (majority, civil capacity, absence of guardianship)
- Their effective ownership right (consistency with the TF)
- The absence of non-consenting co-heirs or co-owners (important in cases of succession, joint ownership)
- The existence and validity of a mandate or power of attorney, where applicable
- The matrimonial regime for married sellers (spouse's consent if necessary)

**How**: direct interview with the seller, documentary verification, cross-checking with civil status registries and the TF.

**Risks covered**: usurper seller, unauthorized seller (lapsed mandate, falsified power of attorney), absence of spouse's consent, latent inheritance disputes.

---

### 4. Physical site visit

**Goal**: verify in person that the physical land matches the documents and is usable.

**What we verify**:

- The correspondence between the physical land and the TF coordinates
- The general state of the land: current occupation, existing constructions, visible anomalies (fences, plantations, occupants)
- The presence and condition of physical boundary markers (where applicable)
- The accessibility of the land (road, track, condition)
- Practical characteristics: general topography, vegetation, water points, connections (electricity, water)
- Systematic photographs of the boundaries, markers, and the land as a whole

**How**: physical site visit by a certified KAMNET Agent, accompanied if necessary by a surveyor. Complete photographic documentation.

**Risks covered**: illegally occupied land, displaced markers, inaccessible land, discrepancies between documents and the reality on the ground.

---

### 5. Neighborhood verification

**Goal**: identify disputes, litigation, or conflicts likely to weaken future ownership.

**What we verify**:

- Any ongoing land disputes on the land or neighboring parcels (judicial proceedings, customary arbitrations)
- Visible easements: passages, access, drainage, connections crossing the land
- Known disputes over ownership by neighbors, former owners, or customary rights holders
- The history of occupation and any customary conflicts (important in rural and peri-urban areas)
- The perception of the land by local authorities (chiefdoms, town halls)

**How**: neighborhood inquiry with neighbors, consultation of local authorities (chiefdom, town hall, urban planning service), cross-checking with the land registry office services.

**Risks covered**: customary disputes, undisclosed ongoing litigation, undeclared easements, latent family conflicts likely to resurface.

---

### 6. Urban planning verification

**Goal**: confirm that the land is usable in line with the client's project (residential, commercial, agricultural, mixed).

**What we verify**:

- The zoning applicable to the land (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, mixed, non aedificandi zone)
- Constructibility (prior authorizations, allowed construction ratios, maximum height)
- The MINDCAF-validated subdivision plan (where applicable, essential for subdivided lands or those undergoing subdivision)
- Public development projects likely to affect the land: roads, public facilities, expropriations
- Distance from protected infrastructure (road, rail, electrical, hydraulic easements)

**How**: consultation of the municipality's urban planning services, verification of the subdivision plan at MINDCAF, analysis of public development plans.

**Risks covered**: non-buildable land sold as buildable, administratively unvalidated subdivision, undisclosed future public project (road, expropriation), restrictive easements.

---

### 7. Chain of ownership verification

**Goal**: trace the history of transfers to ensure that current ownership rests on an unbroken and lawful chain.

**What we verify**:

- Successive transfers recorded on the TF (who sold to whom, on what dates)
- The existence of an unbroken chain of ownership (no "gap" in transfers)
- The absence of known historical disputes (prior proceedings, judgments, amicable settlements)
- The compliance of transfers with OHADA transfer rules and the Cameroonian Land Code
- The absence of suspicious transfers (transfers at derisory prices, closely-spaced transfers, chained transfers without economic logic)

**How**: historical analysis of the TF, cross-checking with available notarial deeds, verification with the land registry office of successive entries.

**Risks covered**: broken chain of ownership, fraudulent transfers, conveyance to an illegitimate seller, money laundering scheme or regulatory circumvention.

## Methodology: minimum 2 physical visits

The KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ mission systematically involves **two physical visits**:

1. **Visit to the competent land registry office** (MINDCAF) for documentary verifications (1, 7) and the recent mortgage status report
2. **Visit to the Land site** for the physical visit (4) and the neighborhood verification (5)

The other verifications (2 cadastral, 3 seller, 6 urban planning) combine documentary work and on-site reconnaissance depending on the case's needs.

<Callout type="warning">
**No 100% remote verification is possible.** Cameroonian land cannot be verified from a Paris or Montreal office. KAMBRIQ accepts this physical demand because it is the only one that truly protects our clients.
</Callout>

## The verification Report

At the end of the 7 verifications, KAMBRIQ delivers to the client a structured **Report**, comprising:

- A Land identification sheet (cadastral references, coordinates, surface area)
- For each of the 7 verifications: conclusion, any observations, identified risk level
- A global synthesis note with final recommendation (favorable, favorable with reservations, unfavorable)
- The supporting documents consulted (photographs, registry extracts, administrative attestations)

### KAMBRIQ risk scale

Each report classifies the land according to an internal scale:

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Controlled** | No significant risk identified - KAMBRIQ green light |
| **Moderate** | Risks identified but manageable with support - amber light |
| **Watch** | Heightened vigilance required, points of attention listed - flashing red light |
| **Not recommended** | Significant legal or administrative risks - red light |

### Delivery time

The indicative delivery time for the Report is **21 working days** from the complete reception of documents and payment. This timeline may be extended in case of administrative delays, particular complexity, or difficulties accessing the land.

## Acknowledged limits

KAMBRIQ acknowledges the limits of its service with transparency. Our method is rigorous, but it falls under a **best-efforts obligation** and not an obligation of result.

### What KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ cannot guarantee

- The absolute absence of hidden defects undetectable by standard verifications
- The absence of sophisticated documentary fraud (perfect forgeries, hidden administrative complicity)
- Lasting legal validity in the event of unforeseeable subsequent judicial dispute
- Future evolution of context (regulation, public projects not yet decided)
- The absence of future disputes with third parties subsequent to the report

### What KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ does not replace

KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ does not substitute for:

- The **notarial office**: only the notary can authenticate the transaction and engage notarial liability for the formal regularity of the deed
- Specific **technical expertise**: geotechnical (constructibility), environmental, archaeological, hydrographic
- **Personalized legal counsel**: for complex situations, KAMBRIQ recommends engaging a Cameroonian lawyer and, where applicable, a lawyer in the client's country of residence

## The contribution of artificial intelligence

KAMBRIQ uses artificial intelligence tools to accelerate certain documentary tasks: comparative plan analysis, archive database searches, automatic report structuring, multilingual translation.

However, **AI never substitutes for the physical verifications** that constitute the core of our method. The realistic gain brought by AI concerns desk tasks (1 to 2 days saved on documentary analysis), not field work, which remains irreducibly human.

This honesty is essential: no technology can visit a parcel, question a neighbor, read a hand-paginated register at a land registry office in Dschang or Douala-Bonabéri. Our strength is the combination of high-performance digital tools and a real physical presence in Cameroon.
