## What KAMBRIQ VERIFY™ checks

Every land submitted goes through 7 control points:

1. **Title deed authenticity** - Is the document genuine? Does it match official registries?
2. **Identity of the actual owner** - Is the seller the owner registered in the land registry?
3. **Known disputes** - Are there any litigation, objections, or competing claims on this land?
4. **Geographic accuracy** - Does the declared location match the cadastral plan and on-site boundary markers?
5. **Complete technical file** - Are the boundary survey, location plan, and cadastral plan compliant?
6. **Current legal status** - Is the land free of any mortgage, seizure, or easement?
7. **Transaction history** - Who sold what, when, and under what conditions?
