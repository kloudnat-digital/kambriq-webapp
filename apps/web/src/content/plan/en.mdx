## How it works

**01 - Reserve with a 5% deposit**

Your land is secured. No one else can reserve it.

**02 - A payment schedule tailored to your capacity**

Your KAMNET agent works with you to set a realistic payment calendar.

**03 - Real-time tracking**

Track your payments and the progress of your file in your client account.

**04 - Completion at the final instalment**

Once payment is complete, the administrative steps are initiated to obtain your title deed.

## What KAMBRIQ PLAN™ is NOT

KAMBRIQ PLAN™ is not a loan.

There are no interest charges, no hidden fees, no late payment penalties.

It is a payment schedule directly with KAMBRIQ.
